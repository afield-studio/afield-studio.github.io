Pillars

Pillars

iPhone app for wellness, on TestFlight with the App Store next

I was tracking sleep, food, symptoms and workouts in four apps that never talked to each other. Pillars puts them side by side and points out the patterns, like bloating that tends to follow a certain kind of meal.

  • Reads sleep, workouts and heart data from Apple Health, without ever writing to it.
  • Log a meal by describing it or taking a photo; on-device AI turns it into foods and portions.
  • Link symptoms to meals and see which patterns keep showing up.
  • Code writes every number and health statement. Apple's on-device model only picks and titles the findings.

Gridiron IQ

Gridiron IQ

Web app for fantasy football, in beta this season

Fantasy football rewards people who follow the NFL all week. Gridiron IQ levels the field for everyone else, with start/sit, waiver and trade calls that spell out their reasoning.

Try Gridiron IQ
  • Works with ESPN and Sleeper leagues, or with no league at all.
  • Ask in plain English: who to start, who to pick up, whether a trade is fair.
  • Every number in an answer comes from real data, never from the model's memory.

Resume Redox

Resume Redox

Web app for job seekers, live and free

I automated my own job search with Claude and Airtable. Every step worked except the last one: turning a tailored draft into a clean resume. Resume Redox is that step, free and built for anyone.

  1. FindA daily search scores roles for fit
  2. PickI choose which to pursue
  3. TailorAI matches the posting to real experience
  4. FormatResume Redox
Open Resume Redox
  • Unlimited resumes; duplicate one and tailor it for each posting.
  • Import an existing PDF or Word resume.
  • Paste a job posting to see which key terms you cover.
  • Free PDF, Word and text downloads. No account, no watermark.

A connector so the AI you already use, like Claude or ChatGPT, can tailor your resume and save the draft straight into Resume Redox, ready to download.
