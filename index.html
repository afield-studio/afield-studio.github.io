Pillars

iPhone app for wellness, on TestFlight with the App Store next

I was tracking sleep, food, symptoms and workouts in four apps that never talked to each other. Pillars puts them side by side and points out the patterns, like bloating that tends to follow a certain kind of meal.

More about Pillars
  • Reads sleep, workouts and heart data from Apple Health, without ever writing to it.
  • Log a meal by describing it or taking a photo; on-device AI turns it into foods and portions.
  • Code writes every number and health statement. Apple's on-device model only picks and titles the findings.

Gridiron IQ

Web app for fantasy football, in beta this season

Fantasy football rewards people who follow the NFL all week. Gridiron IQ levels the field for everyone else, with start/sit, waiver and trade calls that spell out their reasoning.

Resume Redox

Web app for job seekers, live and free

I automated my own job search with Claude and Airtable. Every step worked except the last one: turning a tailored draft into a clean resume. Resume Redox is that step, free and built for anyone.

More about Resume Redox

How my automation works:

  1. A daily search finds roles and scores how well each fits.
  2. I choose which ones to pursue.
  3. AI matches each requirement to my verified experience and asks me about gaps instead of making things up.
  4. The draft goes into Resume Redox to become the final resume.

Today Resume Redox gives you unlimited resumes, import from PDF or Word, a job-posting keyword check, and free PDF, Word and text downloads. Next is a connector so your own AI can tailor and save drafts straight into it.

Life Dashboard

Web and iPhone app for weekly planning, on TestFlight

My week was split across a calendar, a to-do list and a workout app, and things slipped. Life Dashboard is one place to plan the week on Sunday and give every task, workout and office day a time.

More about Life Dashboard
  • A Sunday plan walks through office days, tasks, groceries, workouts and goals.
  • Drag things into time blocks; Today shows what's next each morning.
  • The web app and the native iPhone app share one account and update each other live.

Next, an AI assistant that plans the week with you. Life Dashboard already holds the structured week an assistant needs.
